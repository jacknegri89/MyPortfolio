# Product

Design context for this repository, read by the Impeccable design skill before any visual work.
It is not part of the website (excluded from the deploy in `.vercelignore`).

## Register

brand

## Users

Two kinds of visitor, in this order of priority:

1. Owners of shops, studios and local businesses, and independent professionals, between Lombardy and Canton Ticino. Usually on a phone, not technical, deciding in a minute whether to write. They need to understand what Giacomo does, see that he can do it, and find the contact.
2. Recruiters and technical teams. On a laptop, scanning for proof: real projects, public code, stack, CV.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Giacomo Negri, developer. It sells one thing to clients: custom websites (business site, landing page, restyling). Software projects are shown as proof of skill, not as a service. Success is a written request for a quote, or a recruiter opening the code or the CV.

## Brand Personality

Precise, direct, hand-built. The voice is first person, plain Italian, concrete nouns, no slogans. The page should feel made by one person who cares about details, not assembled from a kit.

Fixed identity, do not replace: near-black background, a single azure accent, Geist for text, Instrument Serif italic for the accented words in headings, Geist Mono only for dates and code-like labels, the giant name with the particle wave in the hero, the horizontal projects section.

## Anti-references

- Template portfolios: three identical cards with an icon on top, bento grids of tags, pill badges with emoji.
- Violet-to-blue gradients, glass panels, glowing dots.
- Marketing filler ("solutions", "leader", "360°"), invented numbers, reviews or clients.
- The words "freelance" and "web designer".
- Client work shown as case studies (owner's decision).

## Design Principles

1. Show the proof next to the claim: every statement points to a screenshot, a demo, a repository or a file.
2. Rows and type before boxes: use cards only for content that is a self-contained object (a project, the internship).
3. One accent, used for meaning: azure marks what is active, done or linked.
4. Motion explains: a line that draws, a list that fills, a filter that answers. No bounce, no decoration that repeats on every section.
5. Never invent: missing data stays out, planned things are labelled as planned.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Text contrast at least 4.5:1, visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets on touch screens, no horizontal scroll from 320px. Everything readable with reduced motion and without JavaScript. axe-core must report zero violations on every page.
